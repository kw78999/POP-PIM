# Citing POP-PIM

The paper title, authors and persistent identifier are not final yet. Until they are, cite the repository and the
exact commit or release you used:

> POP-PIM: popcount-based KV-cache retrieval near memory, with byte-frugal sparse attention (POP-PIM-S/Q).
> Version 0.2.0. https://github.com/kw78999/POP-PIM

When the publication metadata is final, a `CITATION.cff` with the authors, title, DOI/URL and release version will be
added. The retrieval mathematics (sign/magnitude popcount scoring) comes from
[PopKV](https://github.com/kw78999/PopKV); please cite it separately from POP-PIM's hardware contribution.
